Get to know me

Hi, I'm Alex — nice to meet you.

I'm a software engineer and creative developer based in San Francisco, with six years of experience turning ambiguous problems into products people enjoy using. My path started in a college robotics lab, where I fell in love with the moment code stops being abstract and starts moving something in the real world.

Since then I've worked across startups and larger teams, building everything from real-time analytics dashboards to mobile apps used by thousands of people daily. What keeps me going is the same thing that hooked me early on: the small, satisfying click of watching a system come together.

Outside of shipping features, I care a lot about how software is built — mentoring junior engineers, writing about what I learn, and pushing for interfaces that respect people's time and attention.

When I'm not at the keyboard, you'll find me experimenting with a new recipe, chasing a good trail run, or losing an evening to a strategy game.
